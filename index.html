Reliefs that decide the first two years

  • Ulga na start — 6 months without social contributions (health only). Requires no business activity in the previous 60 months and not performing the same services for a former employer.
  • Mały ZUS — 24 months with the social-insurance base reduced to 30% of the minimum wage.
  • Mały ZUS Plus — contributions proportional to actual revenue while previous-year revenue stays at or below PLN 120,000 and the business ran at least 60 days.
  • Contribution holiday — one month a year exempt from social insurance (health still payable); up to 10 insured persons and annual net income within the EUR 2 million equivalent limit.

What a foreign company is actually paying for

Two routes exist to engage someone in Poland from abroad. Registering a Polish entity makes the company the payer of the 19.48–22.14% above plus payroll administration. Or the engagement runs as a B2B contract under the contractor's own Polish sole proprietorship, and those figures are what the contractor carries, with an administrator handling the paperwork between them. Registering an entity to engage one person is rarely the cheaper answer — that is the arithmetic employer-of-record services are built on.

Poly's version of the flow is six stages in one record: service agreement, client B2B contract, invoice preparation and review, the ZUS and PIT calculations shown as separate deduction lines, payout once the client has paid, and the archive. Where a Polish work permit is needed, application support sits in the same setup. Package scope, prices and VAT treatment are published on the site and require confirmation before any engagement starts.
